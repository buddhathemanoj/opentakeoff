# Arcloom

Arcloom is the product name, replacing Kosal Studio. The name combines architecture
with the idea of weaving a brief into a coherent place. The visual identity is a
folded architectural doorway in lavender and warm ivory, with a transparent
negative-space opening. The wordmark is rendered in the locally bundled DM Sans.

The production logo uses the built-in imagegen tool through the imagegen skill.
The selected PNG is stored at `web/src/brand/arcloom-logo.png`. The Mac ICNS,
Windows ICO and 1024-pixel PNG are generated from this same bitmap; no manually
drawn substitute is used. Existing project schemas, browser storage keys and the
Mac profile directory are preserved, so a branding change does not migrate or
reset drawings.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final logo emblem for ARCLOOM, a premium Mac
application for AI architectural design, land planning and construction quantities.
Asset type: a production logo symbol used in a 24-pixel sidebar and app icon. One
singular, distinctive sculptural geometric emblem: two confident folded
architectural planes interlock into an open arch/doorway, with a precise triangular
negative-space aperture and a subtle offset floor-plan corner. It should suggest
architecture, space, and ideas taking form, with the sophistication of an
independent design tool. Smooth clean silhouette, deliberate proportions, softly
bevelled dimensional edges, restrained lavender/periwinkle material with a warm
ivory highlight. Front-facing, compact, centered on a square canvas, occupying
about 75% of the canvas with balanced padding. Genuine transparent background.
No text, no letters, no wordmark, no tagline, no surrounding badge, no mockup, no
scenery, no checkerboard printed into pixels. No small linework, no towers/skyscraper
clip art, no tools, no sparkle, no neural network motifs. Crisp high quality edges,
generous negative space, visually legible when very small. Deliver a single
finished emblem, not a board of alternatives.

## Final cleanup prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this logo emblem only to perfect its edge
quality. Preserve the exact folded architectural doorway design, its proportions,
lavender/periwinkle and ivory materials, and centered square composition. Remove
every rough extraction halo, stray dot, speckle, tiny pixel fragment and irregular
fringe around the silhouette and within the negative-space opening. The entire
exterior and central opening must have genuine clean alpha transparency. Crisp
smooth anti-aliased boundaries, professional production-quality logo, with simple
smooth shaded bevels and no granular surface noise. Do not introduce a background,
checkerboard pattern, text, wordmark, badge, new shapes or shadow outside the logo.
Preserve the same visual identity; this is a targeted cleanup of the supplied emblem.
